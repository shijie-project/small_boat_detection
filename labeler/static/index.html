In the grid: click a crop to select it and centre the canvas on it; drag the box to move it and a corner to resize it; double-click to zoom the canvas in on it.

With predictions loaded: predictions are cyan and dashed (ship-pred) and read-only. In each image, predictions in falling score order are paired with the unpaired annotation they overlap most, if that IoU reaches "Pair IoU". The "Against predictions" filters list the missed annotations, the false alarms and the pairs by IoU; the dashed cyan box in an annotation's crop is its paired prediction. TP / FP / FN in the header count the pairs at IoU ≥ 0.5.
